## Declined

These were decided against; don't re-propose them:
- Copies of characters from other games (e.g. a Liu Kang lookalike), or sprites and sounds taken from Battletoads or Mortal Kombat.
- Nudity and sexual content; BDSM-styled characters.
- A giant red boot attack.
- A spinning tornado attack.
